## About "Wish You Were Here"

"Wish You Were Here" is a digital repository created for John Walsh's ILS-Z 652 "Digital Libraries," a course taught at Indiana University Bloomington. Created and designed by myself, Abigail Fathauer, this repository serves to digitize my collection of 19th century postcards. I have been collecting postcards and antique ephemera since middle school and I strongly believe in the beauty of the postcard as a unique form of communication and art. In many ways, postcards are a dying art. While many people still collect new postcards while traveling, I've found that few actually mail those postcards. This is true of myself, as well. My postcard collection includes modern postcards from my travels across the country and the world, as well as postcards that were gifted to me. Yet, very few of my modern postcards have written notes or were sent by mail. 

As I approached this project, I found myself fascinated by this lost form of communication and soon turned to my collection of 19th century postcards to examine what people were sharing within them. I was especially interested in how people approached their travels and how they communicated their travel experiences with others. "Wish You Were Here" was born of this research query. This repository exists to collect detailed metadata about the postcards in my collection and to make them more widely searchable and shareable for researchers, genealogists, and those who may be generally interested in antique ephemera.

## Create your own Metadata

To create metadata compatible with this CollectionBuilder-Sheets instance the best way to get started is to make of copy of our template in Google Drive:

[CollectionBuilder Metadata Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Uv9ytll0hysMOH1j-VL1lZx6PWvc1zf3L35sK_4IuzI/copy?usp=sharing){:.btn .btn-outline-primary}
{:.text-center}

Alternatively, you can [download the template CSV]({{ '/assets/metadata-template.csv' | relative_url }}) and work on your local machine.
We suggest editing your CSV using [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) Calc, [OpenRefine](https://openrefine.org/), or Google Sheets (and do not suggest using Excel, since Excel's CSV output is not correctly formatted).

Describe your items in your copy of the template, following the [Metadata Guidelines]({{ '/metadata.html' | relative_url }}). 

Once you have items added you can test your metadata by clicking the "Change the Metadata" button above.
